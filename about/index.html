Principles

How I make product decisions.

  1. Understand real needs

    The people who use a product every day show you where it really ends, often in the spreadsheets they keep on the side.

    Debit payments

  2. Choose what to solve, and what not

    A working prototype tests the reasoning before a team commits to a roadmap.

    Smart Retry (prototype)

  3. Align teams to execute

    Configure what changes, share what doesn’t. Twelve markets, one product.

    Servicing platform

  4. Measure and learn

    Success and stop criteria are written before launch, so results end debates instead of starting them.

    Airtime
